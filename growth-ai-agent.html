The gap we are filling

The hard part is not finding more data. It is knowing what deserves pursuit.

Construction opportunities are fragmented across procurement portals, public records, permit signals, documents, spreadsheets, and inboxes. Generic lead tools rarely understand a contractor's trades, service territory, capacity, valuable project types, exclusions, or safe claims. Growth AI Agent is designed to turn that scattered evidence into a prioritized, reviewable pipeline.

Why I started it

Built from real construction-operations friction.

My work with YAM Estimation exposed the cost of fragmented intake, manual research, contractor coordination, and inconsistent handoffs. I saw a practical gap between a raw opportunity database and a defensible go-or-no-go decision. Growth AI Agent is our answer.

A defining tradeoff

We chose defensible decisions over the fastest automation demo.

Rejected shortcut

Hide uncertainty behind a score, invent missing contact data, or let an agent act before a reviewer understands the evidence.

Chosen system

Preserve provenance, expose blockers and confidence, return zero candidates on provider failure, and keep consequential actions approval-gated.

What “a good lead” means here

Fit is explainable. It is never a marketing promise.

The system is designed to surface better-fit opportunities—not guarantee jobs, contracts, replies, or revenue. Every candidate should earn its place in the pipeline through visible evidence.

Trade and scope

Does the work match an approved division, trade, subtrade, and service?

Territory

Is the project inside the contractor's approved operating area?

Capacity and project type

Does the opportunity fit the preferred work, size, timing, and operating constraints?

Evidence strength

Are the source, dates, documents, scope clues, and contact path strong enough to act on?

Exclusions and blockers

Do hard disqualifiers, missing requirements, deadlines, or uncertainty stop the pursuit?

Human judgment

Can a reviewer understand the reasoning, correct it, and own the next action?

Opportunity states

A pipeline that communicates uncertainty.

  1. Ready to PursueStrong fit, sufficient evidence, and a clear next step.
  2. Needs QualificationPromising, but missing evidence or a required decision.
  3. Research SignalUseful timing or market intelligence, not yet an open bid.
  4. Low FitOutside the approved profile or blocked by an explicit rule.

Responsible by design

Evidence before automation.

  • No fake leads: provider or source failure returns no candidate rather than invented data.
  • Profile-governed scope: the system should not silently broaden beyond approved services, trades, territory, or claims.
  • Visible reasoning: qualification includes provenance, confidence, reason codes, missing evidence, and blockers.
  • Tenant-safe foundations: workspace identity, protected data, credentials, and audit history are designed to remain isolated.
  • Human authority: outreach, CRM handoff, commitments, and other consequential actions remain approval-controlled.
